How to read this:
  failed  = requests that returned NULL although memory may have been free (fragmentation!)
  frag %  = 100 * (1 - largest_free_block / total_free_bytes) at the end of the workload
  Our allocator walks a free list (cost grows with the number of FREE blocks); libc uses
  size-class bins + thread caches, so it stays faster when the heap is badly fragmented.
  worst-fit is slowest AND most fragmented: it must scan every free block and shatters big holes.
